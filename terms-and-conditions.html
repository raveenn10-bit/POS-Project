Description of Service

Harsh Apex Retail POS is a specialized software-as-a-service application designed for retail grocers, mini-supermarkets, pharmacies, bookshops, electronics stores, and small-to-medium retail establishments across Sri Lanka.

Core features of the application include:

  • 100% offline cashier billing utilizing client-side browser storage (IndexedDB);
  • Live camera barcode scanning and compatibility with external USB/Bluetooth scanners;
  • Thermal receipt printing supporting ESC/POS standard 58mm and 80mm printers;
  • Customer credit ledger (ණය පොත) with outstanding debt tracking and WhatsApp balance reminders;
  • Real-time stock ledger, low-inventory notifications, and gross/net profit reporting;
  • Bilingual user interface (Sinhala & English) tailored for Sri Lankan retail workflows.

Strict TWO-DEVICE Limit Per Subscription

Each subscription plan strictly entitles the merchant to connect and activate a maximum of TWO (2) physical devices simultaneously (for example, Primary Cashier Counter 1 and a secondary Manager Smartphone or Counter 2).

  • Both devices must belong to the same registered store location.
  • Both devices share the identical expiration timestamp of the parent subscription.
  • Each physical terminal requires a unique Device ID bound to its hardware environment.
  • Attempting to activate more than 2 devices under a single subscription is a violation of this agreement and will result in license revocation.

Device Replacement & Transfer Rules

If a merchant purchases a new smartphone, replaces a cashier PC, or suffers hardware damage, the license may be transferred to a replacement device:

  • The merchant must contact official support with proof of active subscription and provide the new terminal's Device ID;

  • CRITICAL NOTICE: Transferring an activation does NOT automatically transfer the sales records or stock database stored locally on the old hardware. The merchant must restore data from their exported backup file.
