1.0

Business Identity & Scope

This Privacy Policy is issued by Harsh Apex Digital Solutions ("we", "us", or "our"), an IT software entity established in Colombo, Sri Lanka. This policy applies to all merchants, store owners, cashiers, and users accessing our Retail Point of Sale software via web (https://pos.harshapex.com.lk), desktop packaging, or mobile web applications.

We are committed to operating in accordance with internationally recognized data protection principles and Sri Lankan commercial standards, ensuring that retail business data remains secure, private, and under the merchant’s direct custody.

2.0

Offline-First Architecture & Local Data Custody

The fundamental architectural principle of Harsh Apex POS is Offline-First Data Isolation:

  • Local Hardware Storage: Your product catalog, unit cost prices, selling prices, barcode mappings, invoices, sales receipts, customer credit accounts (ණය පොත), and daily profit margins are stored exclusively inside your device's browser IndexedDB and LocalStorage (or local encrypted SQLite in desktop builds).
  • Zero Server-Side Tracking: During day-to-day point of sale operations, sales transactions are processed locally on the client device CPU. Our servers do not intercept, record, or profile what goods you sell or what prices you charge.
  • Physical Merchant Custody: Because the database resides on your smartphone, tablet, or laptop, you retain complete physical custody of your retail business records.
3.0

Registration Data Collected

When you register for a license or communicate with our team, we collect minimal necessary account details:

  • Merchant Full Name: To identify the license holder;
  • Retail Store / Business Name: To label your terminal and license registration;
  • Contact Phone / WhatsApp Number: For dispatching activation codes, critical security alerts, and providing remote setup assistance;
  • Email Address: For digital tax invoices, payment receipts, and formal communications.

We do not collect sensitive biometric personal information, national identity cards, or credit scores.

4.0

Subscription & Payment Records

To administer software access and maintain accounting records, we store:

  • Subscription tier (e.g. Business Pro Monthly LKR 2,500 or Annual Super Saver LKR 24,000);
  • Payments.lk transaction reference number or bank deposit confirmation number;
  • License issue date, activation timestamp, and scheduled expiration date;
  • Current activation status (Active, Trial, Expired, or Transferred).
5.0

Anonymous Device Identifiers & Telemetry

To enforce our strict two-device subscription quota and protect cryptographic licensing tokens against illegal copying, the application computes an anonymous Device ID:

  • Device ID: A cryptographic one-way hash derived from hardware canvas rendering, browser User-Agent, and screen characteristics. It contains no personally identifiable personal data and cannot be reverse-engineered into personal information.
  • Device Label: A friendly user-entered label (e.g., "Counter 1 - Main Till" or "Manager Phone") stored locally to help merchants identify their paired terminals.
  • Heartbeat Check-In: Periodic check-ins (`/api/device-checkin`) transmit solely the Device ID and license signature to verify that license status has not been revoked or transferred.
6.0

Cloud Storage & Multi-Device Sync (When Enabled)

For merchants who explicitly configure multi-device cloud synchronization or remote backup features:

  • Data transmission occurs exclusively via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption directly to our managed database infrastructure hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL with strict Row Level Security) or Google Firebase.
  • Each merchant's data is isolated using cryptographic tenant tokens. No merchant can access or query another store's inventory or sales data.
  • Multi-device synchronization can be deactivated at any time by the merchant in the POS settings tab.

8.0

Zero Cardholder Data Retention Guarantee

💳
Card Security Guarantee: Harsh Apex Retail POS never processes, collects, transmits, or stores credit or debit card numbers, expiration dates, or 3-digit CVV codes.

All online payments are conducted externally via Payments.lk's hosted, secure checkout window or through direct merchant banking transfers (slip upload via WhatsApp). Our web servers and database instances have zero access to cardholder data environments, ensuring 100% compliance with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS).

9.0

Technical & Organizational Security Measures

We maintain comprehensive defense-in-depth security controls:

  • ECDSA P-256 Cryptographic Signatures: All digital license tokens are signed with asymmetric Elliptic Curve Cryptography using private keys kept offline. Tampered tokens are instantly rejected by client terminals.
  • Strict HTTP Security Headers: Production deployments enforce Content Security Policy (CSP), HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), X-Frame-Options: DENY, and X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff.
  • Terminal Owner PIN Lock: Cashier terminals include a built-in 4-digit Owner PIN locking sensitive features (reports, product editing, database export, settings) to protect business privacy from unauthorized floor staff.
10.0

Merchant Rights, Data Export & Erasure

As a merchant using Harsh Apex Retail POS, you retain the following rights:

  • Instant Local Data Export: You can download a complete, unencrypted or encrypted JSON snapshot of your entire database (products, sales, customers, ledger entries) at any time via Settings → Export Backup (apex-pos-backup.json) or Excel spreadsheet export.
  • Complete Local Deletion: You can purge all store data from your terminal at any moment by clearing your browser site storage or executing the factory reset button in Settings.
  • Account Deletion Request: You may request the permanent deletion of your phone number, email, and subscription registration record by emailing chamilka.ch@gmail.com. Records are expunged within 7 business days, except for statutory tax transaction archives required by Sri Lankan commercial law.
