1.0

Nature of Digital POS SaaS Subscription

Harsh Apex Retail POS (accessible at https://pos.harshapex.com.lk) is a proprietary cloud-enabled and offline-first Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) progressive web application engineered by Harsh Apex Digital Solutions, Colombo, Sri Lanka.

Upon purchasing a monthly (LKR 2,500 / 30 days) or annual (LKR 24,000 / 365 days) subscription, fulfillment is accomplished entirely by issuing cryptographically signed ECDSA P-256 digital license activation tokens bound to the merchant's hardware Device ID. Because delivery is intangible and access is rendered immediately upon activation, digital software services are governed by distinct legal characteristics under intellectual property and consumer protection frameworks.

2.0

Free 7-Day Full-Featured Trial Period

To eliminate any financial risk for Sri Lankan merchants, Harsh Apex provides a completely unrestricted 7-day free trial for every new device. No credit card, debit card, or upfront financial commitment is required to start your trial.

During this 7-day period, merchants are strongly encouraged to thoroughly verify all software capabilities in their live operational retail environment, including:

  • Camera barcode and QR scanning accuracy on your smartphones and tablets;
  • Bluetooth and USB 58mm / 80mm thermal receipt printer pairing and printing speeds;
  • Offline checkout stability when Wi-Fi and mobile data connections are disconnected;
  • Customer credit ledger (ණය පොත) management and PDF statement generation;
  • Daily sales, expense tracking, and gross profit analytics calculations.
🛡️
Try Before You Buy: Because our 7-day trial provides 100% of the Pro features, merchants can confirm full hardware compatibility and suitability before paying any subscription fee.
3.0

Refund Eligibility & Case-by-Case Assessment

Harsh Apex Digital Solutions complies with the principles of fair trading and the Consumer Affairs Authority Act No. 09 of 2003 of Sri Lanka. While digital license tokens that have been consumed or used are generally non-refundable, we uphold a transparent, case-by-case review mechanism to safeguard our merchant partners against genuine transactional or technical failures.

Refund applications are carefully investigated by our technical and compliance team based on objective system logs, payment gateway ledger entries, and verifiable evidence.

4.0

Valid Refund Conditions

A full or partial refund will be granted under the following clearly defined circumstances:

Condition Description Remedy
4.1 Duplicate or Accidental Payment The merchant was billed multiple times for the same subscription period due to a payment gateway latency, network interruption, or browser double-submission. 100% Refund of the duplicate transaction amount back to the originating bank account or card.
4.2 Payment Deducted Without Successful Activation Funds were successfully debited by Payments.lk or bank transfer, but our system failed to generate or deliver a valid activation code, and our technical support team could not resolve the issue within 24 hours of receiving the merchant's notice. 100% Full Refund or optional free license extension based on merchant preference.
4.3 Verified Technical Activation Failure The POS application exhibits a critical, reproducible defect preventing basic cashier checkout operations on a device meeting our documented system requirements, which our support engineering cannot resolve or patch within 48 hours. Pro-rated or Full Refund for the unused subscription term.
5.0

Non-Refundable Scenarios & Limitations

Refunds shall not be granted in the following scenarios:

  • Change of Mind / Premature Business Closure: Decisions to stop using the POS software after activating the paid license, change of merchant retail concept, or voluntary closure of the retail shop.
  • Incompatible Third-Party Hardware: Inability to print caused by non-standard, unbranded, or physically damaged thermal printers, faulty Bluetooth adapters, or unsupported hardware, where the software's standard ESC/POS or browser printing functionality is fully operational.
  • Failure to Utilize 7-Day Trial: Claims regarding lack of features that were readily ascertainable during the free 7-day evaluation window.
  • Lapsed Claim Window: Refund claims submitted more than 14 calendar days (fourteen days) following the payment transaction date.
  • Violations of Terms of Service: Software tampering, reverse engineering, attempting to forge ECDSA license signatures, or illegal business activities.
⚠️
Hardware Compatibility Notice: Harsh Apex POS is compatible with standard ESC/POS Bluetooth and USB thermal printers (58mm and 80mm). Please verify your printer during your 7-day free trial or contact our support team for free pre-purchase setup assistance.
6.0

Refund Request Procedure

To request a refund under an eligible condition, follow these steps:

  1. Contact Official Support: Send a written formal request via email to chamilka.ch@gmail.com or via verified WhatsApp to +94 77 066 3154.
  2. Provide Mandatory Documentation:
    • Merchant Store Name and Registered Owner Name;
    • Payments.lk Transaction Reference ID or Bank Deposit Slip;
    • Unique Device ID displayed on the POS Activation / Settings screen;
    • Date and exact amount of the transaction in LKR;
    • Detailed explanation of the failure or duplicate debit, including screenshots or error logs.
7.0

Review, Investigation & Processing Timelines

We believe in honest, realistic timeframes rather than misleading instant refund claims:

  • Initial Acknowledgement: Within 1 to 2 business days of ticket submission.
  • Technical Investigation: Within 3 business days to cross-check gateway records, device logs, and activation status.
  • Disbursement of Funds: If approved, the refund instruction is submitted to Payments.lk or our commercial bank within 3 to 7 business days.
  • Bank Processing: Depending on the cardholder's issuing bank in Sri Lanka (e.g., Commercial Bank, Sampath Bank, HNB, BOC), the funds will appear on the merchant's card statement or bank account within 3 to 10 additional banking days.
8.0

Manual Monthly Renewal Model (Zero Surprise Re-billing)

Harsh Apex Retail POS strictly enforces a Manual Renewal Architecture:

  • Your debit or credit card is never charged automatically on a recurring schedule.
  • There are no hidden recurring subscriptions, automatic renewals, or cancellation fees.
  • If you do not wish to continue using the software for the following month, you simply do not make the next monthly payment.
  • Your local database (products, sales history, customer credit records) remains 100% safe on your device even after license expiry.
