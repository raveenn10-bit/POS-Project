Important Payment Status Disclosure: Sandbox Mode

📢
Merchant Transparency Disclosure: Our integration with Payments.lk is currently operating in Sandbox Mode while our commercial merchant account undergoes final regulatory review and onboarding verification.

During this transitional compliance period:

  • Live merchant activations are fulfilled securely via direct bank transfer / commercial cash deposit or official Payments.lk checkout link where activated.
  • Merchants simply send their bank deposit slip or transfer confirmation receipt alongside their POS Device ID to our verified business WhatsApp (+94 77 066 3154).
  • Our operations team manually validates the deposit with our bank records and generates the cryptographically signed activation license within 5 to 30 minutes.
3.0

Manual Monthly Renewal Model (No Unwanted Auto-Debits)

We recognize that small retail merchants value financial predictability and control. Harsh Apex POS enforces a Strict Manual Renewal Model:

  • No Saved Cards: Our software never stores your credit card, debit card, or CVC code on our servers.
  • No Automated Recurring debits: You will never be charged without your explicit manual action.
  • Transparent Notifications: A countdown clock is displayed on your POS screen and in Settings, notifying you of your remaining license duration in days, hours, and minutes.
4.0

Trial Expiration & Local Data Safety Guarantee

🛡️
Data Safety Guarantee: When your 7-day free trial or monthly subscription expires, your local business data is NEVER deleted, wiped, or modified.

When a trial or subscription reaches its expiration date:

  • The terminal displays an activation gate requesting a renewal code before opening new billing transactions;
  • All your stored products, wholesale cost prices, selling prices, barcode registers, customer credit records (ණය පොත), and past invoice histories remain completely intact in your device's local database;
  • You can enter a valid renewal code at any time to instantly unlock full billing operations without any data reconfiguration.
5.0

Subscription Expiry & Emergency Backup Access

In the event that your license expires while you are waiting to renew:

  • Your terminal data is never locked away from you permanently. You retain the ability to export an encrypted backup file (apex-pos-backup.json) or access historical records upon requesting temporary administrative assistance.
  • There is no expiration penalty fee or reconnection penalty. Whenever you choose to renew, your billing continues smoothly from your existing product database.
6.0

Multi-Device Rules & 2-Device Quota

Every paid subscription (Rs. 2,500/mo or Rs. 24,000/yr) includes authorization for up to TWO (2) devices:

  • Device Allocation: Typically utilized as Primary Counter Terminal 1 (e.g. Counter PC / Tablet) and Secondary Terminal 2 (e.g. Owner Smartphone or Second Till).
  • Shared Expiration: Both devices share the same 30-day or 365-day subscription expiration date.
  • Independence of Local Storage: Please note that in offline-first mode, product and stock changes made on one phone do not automatically synchronize to the second phone unless cloud multi-device sync is explicitly configured.
7.0

Failed Transactions, Overpayments & 14-Day Dispute Window

If you encounter any payment error, duplicate deduction, or incorrect bank debit:

  • Report the incident to our billing desk within fourteen (14) calendar days of the transaction date.
  • Provide your bank slip or transaction SMS showing the reference number and deducted amount in LKR.
  • Our billing team will trace the funds with Payments.lk or our bank and resolve the discrepancy within 24 to 48 hours, either by immediately issuing the correct license or executing a full refund in accordance with our Refund Policy.
8.0

Cancellation Anytime Without Penalties or Fees

Merchants have the absolute freedom to discontinue service at any time:

  • Because renewals are manual, there is no need to navigate complex cancellation forms or call customer care to prevent future charges;
  • Simply do not purchase a renewal code for the upcoming 30-day cycle;
  • There are zero cancellation fees, zero penalties, and zero early termination costs.
