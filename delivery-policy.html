Step-by-Step Digital Activation & Delivery Flow

Obtaining and activating your subscription takes six straightforward steps:

  1. Open the Web Terminal: Visit pos.harshapex.com.lk on your cash counter phone, tablet, or PC.
  2. Copy your Device ID: The terminal generates an anonymous, hardware-bound Device ID visible on the activation screen or in Settings → Device Details. Click the "Copy" button.
  3. Execute Payment: Pay the subscription fee (Business Pro Monthly: LKR 2,500 / 30 days or Annual Super Saver: LKR 24,000 / year) via Payments.lk checkout link or direct commercial bank transfer.
  4. Submit Details via WhatsApp: Send your payment slip / reference ID along with your copied Device ID to our official WhatsApp support line at +94 77 066 3154.
  5. Receive Cryptographic Activation Code: Our automated licensing engine validates the payment and issues an asymmetric ECDSA P-256 activation token specifically cryptographically signed for your device.
  6. Activate and Start Billing: Paste the activation code into the terminal activation dialog and click "Activate POS". The terminal unlocks immediately with your full subscription active.

6.0

Device Replacement & Re-Delivery Procedure

If a merchant upgrades to a new counter tablet or replaces a damaged phone during their active paid subscription:

  • Digital re-delivery of a replacement activation code is provided free of charge;
  • Merchant provides the new Device ID along with their registered store phone number;
  • Our administrators transfer the remaining subscription days to the new terminal and revoke the old device binding in compliance with our 2-device policy;
  • Turnaround time for replacement re-delivery is 15 to 45 minutes during business hours.
7.0

System & Hardware Requirements for Digital Delivery

To successfully receive and run the web application, your terminal must meet these standard criteria:

  • Supported Web Browsers: Google Chrome (v90+), Microsoft Edge (v90+), Apple Safari (v14+), or Mozilla Firefox.
  • Operating Systems: Android 8.0 or higher, iOS / iPadOS 14 or higher, Windows 10 / 11, macOS, Linux, or ChromeOS.
  • Hardware: Minimum 2 GB RAM (4 GB recommended), functional camera for barcode scanning, and Bluetooth 4.0+ or USB OTG for thermal printer connectivity.
  • Network: Standard internet connection required only for initial page load and activation; subsequent POS cashier operations run 100% offline.
